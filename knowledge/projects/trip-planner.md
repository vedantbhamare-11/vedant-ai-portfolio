# Trip Planner — archived

**Link:** https://github.com/vedantbhamare-11/Trip-Planner

## Status

Archived. Not featured in the portfolio and not referenced in the project index beyond a
note. Retained here so the record is complete.

## Overview

A travel itinerary generator. Users enter a destination, duration and travel style, and the
app produces a daily schedule with recommended locations, hotel booking links and a
printable PDF export.

## My contribution

All of it.

## Technologies I personally used

Next.js, TypeScript, Material UI, the Geoapify Places API, Unsplash image fetching, PDF
export.

## Engineering notes

Geoapify supplied categorised place recommendations — entertainment, nature, heritage —
from which the app generated four to five locations per day. A drag-and-drop interface let
users rearrange the schedule, and itineraries exported to PDF for offline use.

## Why archived

The technical story is mostly third-party API integration, which is a thinner demonstration
than the tier-1 projects. It is a working application, not a weak one, but it is not what I
should lead with.
