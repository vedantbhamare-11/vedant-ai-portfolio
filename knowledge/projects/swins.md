# Project: SWINS

* **Title:** SWINS
* **Description:** A gamified, real-time peer appreciation social platform built for enterprises to drive company culture, engagement, and employee retention.
* **Technologies:** React Native, Redux, Node.js, REST APIs, MongoDB, WebSockets
* **Link:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.swins

## Deep Dive & Architectural Context for AI Knowledge

I engineered SWINS to replace boring, top-down "Employee of the Month" awards with a continuous, peer-to-peer social appreciation feed. I built the mobile client with React Native and Redux, supported by a Node.js and MongoDB backend.

To make the platform engaging, I integrated WebSockets for a live, scrollable feed and instant push notifications. When users appreciate a colleague, they select specific achievement types and write personalized messages. Both the sender and receiver earn points that feed into real-time company leaderboards and unlock milestone badges. These points can then be redeemed in a company store. This project proved that enterprise tools can be as intuitive and addictive as consumer social media, driving measurable improvements in team morale and retention.
