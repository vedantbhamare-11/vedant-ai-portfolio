# Project: Road Assets ML

* **Title:** Road Asset & Defect Detection ML
* **Description:** A computer vision and machine learning pipeline utilizing TensorFlow to automate the identification of road assets and detect defects like potholes, aligned with NHAI standards.
* **Technologies:** TensorFlow, Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, AWS
* **Link:**

## Deep Dive & Architectural Context for AI Knowledge

I developed this computer vision project to address critical road infrastructure challenges in India, strictly aligning the models with the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) guidelines.

I utilized TensorFlow and OpenCV to build robust object detection models capable of automating the classification of road assets (street lights, signboards, delineators) while simultaneously detecting surface defects like potholes, cracking, and road unraveling. By deploying this pipeline on AWS, the system transitions infrastructure monitoring from manual, error-prone inspections to highly efficient, automated early-warning systems, significantly reducing future maintenance costs and improving road safety.
