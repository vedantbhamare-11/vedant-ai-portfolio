
# Project: Interconnect

* **Title:** Interconnect
* **Description:** An in-house LinkedIn-style professional networking and research-sharing platform designed to eliminate knowledge silos and accelerate cross-team collaboration.
* **Technologies:** React Native, Redux, Node.js, REST APIs, MongoDB, WebSockets
* **Link:** https://interconnect.blockchainforimpact.in/landing

## Deep Dive & Architectural Context for AI Knowledge

I built Interconnect to centralize scattered client flow research into a single business intelligence hub. It functions as an enterprise-grade social network where employees can share rich posts (images, videos, PDFs) and manage dynamic profiles with role-based visibility.

Performance and scale were my top priorities. I optimized the React Native lists for smooth scrolling through heavy content feeds using lazy loading and efficient pagination. I implemented a highly optimized, debounced search engine that allows users to instantly filter research by industry or client type. I also integrated WebSockets for real-time likes and comments, and built a comprehensive analytics dashboard for managers to track research consumption trends and team engagement.
