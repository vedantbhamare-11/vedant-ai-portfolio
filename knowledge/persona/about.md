# About Vedant

Vedant is a Frontend Developer and UI Engineer based in Bangalore, India. He specializes in building modern, AI-first web applications and creating highly polished, responsive user interfaces.

His coding journey began in 2017 with his first lines of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. He officially formalized his education by completing his Computer Engineering degree (2019 - 2023), establishing a strong foundation in Python, Java, and web development architectures.

Today, Vedant merges his expertise in modern frontend frameworks (React, Next.js, TypeScript) with cutting-edge AI integrations (LLMs, Gen AI, Prompt Engineering) to build platforms that are both visually stunning and highly functional.

Beyond engineering, Vedant is an avid photographer with a keen eye for visual composition, maintaining an active portfolio of both landscape and portrait photography.
