# About Vedant Bhamare

Vedant Bhamare is a software developer with roughly 18 months of full-time engineering
experience, most recently as a Software Developer at TMCC in Chennai (September 2024 –
February 2026). He holds a B.Tech in Computer Engineering from Mumbai University.

His depth is in frontend and product engineering — React, Next.js, React Native and
TypeScript — and he is actively expanding into full-stack work and AI application
engineering. He is not currently employed and is open to roles anywhere in India.

## Positioning

Vedant targets **Full-stack / Product Engineer roles with an AI focus** and **AI
Application Engineer roles**.

He should not be positioned as a pure frontend developer, which understates the backends
he has written, nor as a model-training or research-oriented AI engineer, which he has no
production experience in.

## What the evidence supports

- **Frontend and mobile engineering.** Shipped web and mobile applications across React,
  Next.js and React Native, including two Android apps on the Play Store. This is where his
  depth is.
- **AI application engineering.** Three solo, live products built around LLM APIs, with
  prompt design, output guardrails, streaming, and multimodal input handling.
- **End-to-end ownership on smaller systems.** Rider Tracker was built entirely by him,
  frontend through backend to production deployment. AI Curriculum's Express backend,
  BullMQ workers and Redis queues are also solo work.
- **Working inside a team system.** At TMCC he more often owned the frontend of systems
  whose backends were built by others, contributing selected endpoints where needed.

## Where he is still developing

Automated testing, CI/CD beyond auto-deploy, raw SQL, and monitoring and observability are
genuine gaps. System design at production scale is something he has read about more than
practised. Algorithmic interview preparation is in progress.

## Working style

Vedant learns primarily by building. He favours understanding an existing architecture
before changing it, and he holds reasonably high standards for code structure and clarity.
He mentored interns at WOFO on clean code, modular architecture, React and Git.

He writes technical documentation and data-flow diagrams for his own projects, and his
explanatory writing tends toward approachable rather than formal.
